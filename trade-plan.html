How each level is calculated
—
1. Take the live price (or last daily close). 2. Multiply the measured ATR% by that price to get an absolute ATR: ATR = price × ATR% / 100. 3. Offset the stops and targets by fixed ATR multiples, so every instrument on the sheet carries the same risk geometry regardless of its price scale. R:R is therefore constant at 1:1.25 (TP1) and 1:2.08 (TP2); what changes per instrument is the volatility and the bias, not the structure.
Trend / signal come from the currency-bias model (forex, from the two currencies in the pair) and the macro-bias table (crypto, commodities). Timeframe widens on high-impact event days because the first spike usually reverses. Malaysia time column gives the next event that matters to that instrument, in MYT (GMT+8).
ATR data: — — real daily OHLC, Wilder smoothing. FX and commodities: Yahoo Finance. Crypto: Coinbase Exchange candles.
Not financial advice. These are systematic, ATR-derived templates generated from published bias and measured volatility. They are not recommendations, they do not know your position size or risk limits, and levels go stale as the market moves — re-read them against a live chart before acting. Commodity rows are futures contracts (a basis vs spot exists).
